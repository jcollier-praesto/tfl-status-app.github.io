# Support

For general questions, bug reports, or accessibility feedback, start a
discussion in the repository's [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/jcollier-praesto/tfl-status-app/discussions).

Discussions are public. Do not include personal, confidential, location, or
sensitive information. This project does not currently provide a private
contact route for privacy or data-protection requests. The app stores settings
in your browser; the [privacy page](./privacy.html) includes a control to clear
those settings and local offline caches.
